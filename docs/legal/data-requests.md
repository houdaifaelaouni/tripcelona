# Data requests (access, correction, deletion)

People write to the site email to use their GDPR rights. Answer within one
month. Keep the request email as proof of what was asked and when.

## Deletion ("borrad mis datos")

1. Find everything for that email: Admin > Reservas (search the email),
   Admin > Solicitudes (presupuestos and mensajes).
2. Open each item and use **Protección de datos > Anonimizar datos**.
   - Messages and quote requests: name, email, phone, message and notes are
     wiped.
   - Bookings: only once the booking is cancelled, refunded or the excursion
     has happened. Name, email, phone and notes are wiped, as are the payment
     provider's stored notification for that booking. The reference, amount
     and dates stay for the accounts (6 years, see the privacy policy).
3. Outside this site:
   - **Stripe** (if it was the provider): Dashboard > Customers or Payments,
     search the email, and use "Delete customer" or ask Stripe support to
     redact the data. Stripe keeps payment records it is legally required to
     keep.
   - **PAYCOMET / Redsys**: they keep card transaction records under banking
     rules; no action unless the person asks, then ask the bank.
   - **Resend**: email logs expire on their own after the plan's retention
     period; for an earlier wipe, delete the emails in the Resend dashboard.
   - **Your own mailbox**: delete the alert emails and replies about that
     person.
4. Reply to the person saying what was deleted and what is kept by law.

## Access or copy of the data

Open the same items in the admin and send the person what is stored about
them (the facts on each page), or a CSV from Admin > Exportar with only
their rows kept.

## Correction

Edit the booking or request in the admin, or reply asking for the right
details.
